# Tiered Research Roadmap

## Tier 1
- **Impact Labels:** Hazard proxies (e.g. water depth) don't map to human-scale infrastructure impacts. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Non-stationarity:** Static models decay under climate drift and seasonal shifts. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Physics-AI:** Pure ML models violate kinematic laws during extreme out-of-distribution shocks. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Alert Fatigue:** Static probability thresholds cause 'flapping' in operational UIs. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Explainability:** SHAP/feature importance plots can be mathematically plausible but unreliable. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Benchmark:** Literature claims are incomparable due to shifting datasets and leakage. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Rare Events:** Standard metrics hide extreme failure modes in safe background data. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Decision Uncertainty:** Deterministic warnings force operators to guess confidence bounds. *(Status: Addressed)*

## Tier 2
- **Sensor Placement:** Municipalities lack budget to instrument every node optimally. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Missing-data Recovery:** Telemetry streams fail during extreme events (burst dropouts), crashing ML models. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Downscaling:** Coarse satellite grids mask sub-grid infrastructure impacts. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Action Optimization:** Probabilistic forecasts do not natively map to asymmetric real-world financial costs. *(Status: Partial)*
- **Cybersecurity:** Cloud-connected telemetry is vulnerable to hostile injection. *(Status: Addressed)*
- **Governance:** Research code lacks auditability and version control for critical alerts. *(Status: Addressed)*

## Tier 3
- **Equity:** Warning systems ignore socio-economic vulnerability. *(Status: Open)*
- **Recovery Forecasting:** Focus is entirely on failure onset, not system restoration. *(Status: Open)*
